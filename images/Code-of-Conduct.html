Code of Conduct

Our core principles for ethical leadership, client trust, financial discipline, and secure operations.

1. People Management

  1. Leadership authority shall never override truth, quality, safety, ethics, accountability, or compliance obligations.
  2. Leaders shall lead by example and promote open, transparent, and professional communication. Video participation in official virtual meetings is encouraged whenever practical and appropriate.
  3. Retaliation against employees, contractors, or stakeholders who raise concerns in good faith shall not be tolerated.
  4. Leaders shall mentor, coach, and support employees through constructive feedback, continuous learning, and professional development.
  5. Leaders shall treat all employees, customers, suppliers, and partners with dignity, fairness, professionalism, and respect.

2. Marketing & Finance Principles

  1. Customer trust, ethical business practices, and long-term relationships shall take precedence over short-term gains.
  2. Marketing communications shall be truthful, accurate, and free from misleading claims.
  3. The organization shall strive to achieve a target Net Profit %, calculated as:
    Net Profit = Revenue − (Direct Costs + Operating Expenses + Taxes)
  4. Financial decisions shall support sustainable growth, quality delivery, employee development, and business continuity.

3. Working Hours

  • Core Working Hours: Monday to Friday, 8 hours per day.
  • Regular Weekly Holiday: Sunday.
  • Saturday: Available for business enquiries, customer support, training, project commitments, or special business needs as applicable.

4. Basic IT & Information Security Practices

  1. Access & Authentication: Use strong, unique passphrases and enable multi-factor authentication on all business accounts. Never share credentials, and lock screens whenever stepping away from a workstation.
  2. Data Protection & Confidentiality: Store client, project, and personal data only in approved, access-controlled repositories. Never transfer proprietary data to unauthorized devices or public drives, and never bring confidential materials from prior employers or unauthorized third parties into the work environment.
  3. Licensed & Approved Software: Install and run only authorized, properly licensed software and tools. Avoid unverified applications or browser extensions that introduce security or legal risks.
  4. Updates & Endpoint Hygiene: Keep operating systems, applications, and security defenses updated with current patches. Avoid connecting untrusted external media, and verify sources before opening links or attachments.
  5. Acceptable Use & External Tools: Use company systems and network resources responsibly and lawfully. Do not paste confidential client code, contracts, or personal data into public AI tools or unapproved external services.
  6. Backups & Incident Reporting: Maintain regular backups of critical project artifacts, and report suspected phishing, malware, data leaks, or lost devices immediately.
Core Principle: Leadership authority shall never override truth, quality, safety, ethics, accountability, or compliance obligations.

Compliance & Reporting: All team members, contractors, and partners are expected to uphold this Code of Conduct. Any suspected violations or ethical concerns may be reported directly to leadership in good faith without fear of retaliation.
